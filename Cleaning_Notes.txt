   Issue:  4 different date formats; some dates are ambiguous

6. Wrong data types
   Issue:  transaction_date and created_at are stored as text.
   Action: Convert to datetime.

7. Negative amounts
   Issue:  About 50% of amounts are negative in every transaction

9. Unreliable created_at
   Issue:  All values are in 2023; 62% are before the transaction date.
   Action: Drop created_at; use transaction_date for time analysis.

10. Multiple currencies
   Issue:  Amounts are in 5 currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, PLN).
   Action: Analyze amounts per currency (do not sum across currencies).

11. Unnecessary columns

           across customers.
   Action: Drop ip_address and customer_name; use customer_id.
